## Frequently Asked Questions

### Do I need internet to chat?
No. Once a model is downloaded, LokalMind works completely offline.
Internet is only used to download models and app updates.

### Why is the app slow on my device?
Larger models (4B) need more RAM. Try a smaller model like Qwen 3.5 0.8B
for faster responses on older devices.

### How do I delete a model?
Go to the Models tab → tap the model → Delete.
This frees up storage immediately.

### Where is my chat data stored?
Everything - chats, settings, memories - is stored locally on your device.
Nothing is sent to any server.

### What is a system prompt?
A system prompt tells the AI how to behave - its tone, expertise, and rules.
LokalMind lets you write your own for each mode in Profile → AI Behavior.

### The model failed to load. What do I do?
Try closing and reopening the app. If it persists, delete and re-download
the model from the Models tab.

### How do I reset everything?
Profile → Erase All Data. This wipes all chats, settings, memories,
and downloaded models from your device.
