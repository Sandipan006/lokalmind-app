# LokalMind

**Your Private AI, Always Offline**

*Also: Your mind. Your device. No cloud.*

---

## What it is

**LokalMind** is a mobile app that runs powerful AI chat **on your phone**. You choose and download open models to your device; answers are generated **locally**, not on a remote chat server. After models are on your device, you can use the app **without an internet connection** for conversation—connection is mainly for downloading models and app updates.

It is built for people who want **privacy**, **offline use**, and **control over which model** they run—without sending every prompt to a company’s cloud for the core chat experience.

---

## Why it’s different

Most AI assistants send your messages to the internet, run them on someone else’s computers, and depend on their accounts, pricing, and policies.

LokalMind is the opposite for the **local chat loop**: inference happens **on your device**. Your prompts and replies for those runs stay on the phone. You are not “renting” access to a single remote model—you **pick from a catalog**, download what fits your phone, and switch when you want a different trade-off of speed versus capability.

LokalMind does not try to replace every cloud AI scenario (largest frontier models, team workspaces, web-only tools). It focuses on **private, portable, you-own-the-weights** chat on a phone.

## What you can do

**Chat & sessions**

- Start and keep **multiple conversations**, with a recents list and search across titles and messages.
- **Stream** answers as they are written, stop mid-generation, and **switch models** within a session when you have more than one downloaded.
- **Queue messages** while the model is still replying—see them in a strip, edit or remove before they send.
- **Edit** a past user message: the thread **rewinds** from there so you can correct and continue.
- Optional **auto-titles** so sessions stay easy to scan.

**Models**

- Browse a catalog of **open-weight models** in different sizes (balance of speed and quality).
- **Download** over the network with **pause, resume, and cancel**; **delete** models to free space.
- On first setup, the app helps **match model size to your device’s memory** so you are not pushed into downloads that cannot run well.

**How the assistant behaves**

- **Chat modes**—such as general, coding, creative, and marketing—adjust tone and focus.
- **Your own system prompts** — In Profile, you can **write or change the system prompt for each mode**: define exactly how the assistant should behave for that mode (expertise, tone, rules). **Leave it blank** to use the app’s built-in default for that mode. Changes apply to new messages in that mode going forward.

**Reading answers**

- Answers support **rich text**: emphasis, lists, and **code blocks** with copy.
- When a model shows extended “thinking” in a structured way, the interface can **separate** that from the main answer so the final reply stays easy to read.

**Your space**

- **Profile** with display name and avatar, plus links to support the project or get in touch when the app is configured for them.
- **Erase all local data** in one place—chats, settings, and downloaded models—useful if you sell the device or want a clean slate.

**Experience**

- **Dark, glass-style** interface, **haptics** on key actions, and a layout meant to feel at home on **iOS**—premium and readable, not a generic web box.

## Upcoming features

Planned chat improvements (from the current product roadmap):

- **Reply threading** — Quote-style replies: show a referenced snippet above your message, with a gesture (e.g. swipe or long-press) to reply to a specific bubble—similar to familiar messenger apps.
- **Share or export chats** — Export a full conversation as **PDF** and/or **Markdown** (exact format and flow to be finalized).
- **Regenerate with history** — Regenerate an assistant answer while **keeping earlier versions** of that turn; **arrow controls** to move between previous and new replies without losing context.

**Later ideas** — Pin or bookmark important messages; **remember draft text** in the composer per chat session when you leave and come back.

---

## Honest boundaries

Some capabilities are **still evolving**: for example, **voice input** may be prepared under the hood before the full hold-to-talk experience ships; **Android** may follow after iOS is solid; and features like **global memory** or **attachments** may grow over time. The app is honest about being **local-first**—the value is privacy and offline use, not parity with every cloud product.

---

## In short

LokalMind is for anyone who wants **AI chat that stays on the device**, works **offline once models are downloaded**, and leaves **you in charge of models and data**—with a focused, polished mobile experience around that promise.
