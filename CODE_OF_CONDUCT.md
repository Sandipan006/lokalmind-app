# Code of Conduct

LokalMind follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, welcome newcomers, give and accept feedback kindly, and do not harass anyone. Maintainers may remove comments, commits or contributions that break these rules, and may ban repeat offenders.

## Reporting

Report problems privately to sandipanhiru@gmail.com. Reports are handled in confidence.
