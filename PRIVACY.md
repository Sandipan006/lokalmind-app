# LokalMind - Privacy Policy

*Last updated: April 2026*

---

## The short version

LokalMind is built on a single principle: **your data never leaves your device.**

No accounts. No tracking. No ads. No cloud inference.
Your conversations are yours - stored locally, processed locally, deleted locally.

> **Privacy Promise:** Local-first by default. Your chat data is never uploaded.

---

## What we collect

**Nothing.**

LokalMind does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on external servers.

---

## What stays on your device

The following data is stored **only on your iPhone**, in local SQLite and
device storage:

- Your chat messages and conversation history
- Downloaded AI model files (.gguf)
- Your display name and avatar preference
- Your custom system prompts
- Your memory notes and learned profile
- App settings and preferences

None of this is accessible to us, any third party, or any remote server.

---

## Network usage

LokalMind uses the internet **only** for:

- Downloading AI model files from our CDN (Hetzner)
- Receiving app updates via Expo OTA

**Your chat prompts and responses are never sent over the internet.**
AI inference happens entirely on your device using downloaded models.

---

## Third-party services

| Service | Purpose | Data shared |
|---|---|---|
| Hetzner CDN | Model file downloads | None - anonymous file requests only |
| Expo OTA | App JS bundle updates | None - standard app update mechanism |
| RevenueCat | In-app purchases (if applicable) | Purchase receipt only - no chat data |

---

## Children's privacy

LokalMind does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.
Since no data is collected at all, there is nothing to protect by age category.

---

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be committed to this
repository with a new date. Significant changes will be noted in the
app release notes.
